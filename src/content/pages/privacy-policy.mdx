This Privacy Policy describes how **Staying Cosy** (“we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you visit our website **stayingcosy.com.**

**1. Information We Collect**

We collect your **name** and **email address** when you:

- Subscribe to our mailing list
- Download a free digital product

**2. Why We Collect Your Information**

We collect this information so we can:

- Send you our monthly newsletter
- Share updates, promotions, and relevant design content

**3. How We Collect Information**

We gather this data when you fill out:

- Sign-up forms
- Subscribe forms
- Email collection forms related to free product downloads

**4. How We Use Your Information**

Your information is used solely for our own website-related communications. We do **not** share or sell your data to third parties.

**5. How We Share Information**

We use your name and email to send monthly newsletters and occasional updates. These are sent directly to the email address you provided.

**6. How Long We Keep Your Information**

We store your data for as long as you remain subscribed. You can unsubscribe at any time by clicking the unsubscribe link at the bottom of any of our emails.

**7. Cookies**

Our website uses cookies to enhance your browsing experience. A cookie notice will appear when you first visit our site. For a full breakdown of the cookies used, please refer to our [**Cookie Policy**](/cookie-policy).

**8. Your Rights**

You may request access to the personal data we hold about you, or request that we delete or correct it, at any time. Just email us at [**info@stayingcosy.com**](mailto:info@stayingcosy.com).
