We use cookies to improve your experience on our website. Cookies are small data files stored on your device. Some are necessary to help the site function properly, while others help us understand how you use the site or show you more relevant content.

**Types of Cookies We Use:**

- **Necessary Cookies** (always on): These are essential for the website to function and cannot be switched off.
- **Performance & Analytics Cookies**: These help us understand how visitors use our website so we can improve its design and functionality.
- **Advertising Cookies**: These may be used by advertising partners to deliver ads more relevant to you.

You can manage your cookie preferences through the pop-up window when you first visit the site, or by adjusting your browser settings.

If you have any questions, please contact us at [**info@stayingcosy.com**](mailto:info@stayingcosy.com).
